What is a Photovoltaic (PV) System?

Photovoltaic Effect — Turning Light into Electricity

A photovoltaic (PV) system converts sunlight directly into electricity using the photoelectric effect. When photons from sunlight strike a semiconductor material (usually silicon), they knock electrons loose, creating a direct current (DC). An inverter then converts this DC into the alternating current (AC) that powers homes, factories, and grids. The Kaggle dataset used in this project is from a real solar plant in India, recording irradiance, panel temperatures, and AC power output at 15-minute intervals over 34 days.

Solar Irradiation

The amount of solar energy reaching the panel surface (W/m²). Higher irradiation = more photons = more electrons excited = more power. Clouds, dust, and angle of incidence all reduce effective irradiation. In this dataset it is normalised to a 0–1 scale.

Module Temperature

Counter-intuitively, higher panel temperature reduces efficiency. Silicon panels lose roughly 0.4–0.5% efficiency per °C above 25 °C. This is why deserts produce less power than expected — peak sun comes with peak heat. Cooling systems or bifacial panels help.

AC Power Output

The final usable power delivered to the grid (kW). This is the target variable our ML model predicts. It accounts for inverter losses (typically 2–5%), wiring resistance, and mismatch losses between panels in a string. Plant 1 in this dataset peaks around 3,000 kW.

Single-Diode Model

The physics-based model used for validation. It treats a solar cell as a diode with a photocurrent source. Five parameters (IL, I0, Rs, Rsh, n) fully describe the I-V curve. Our ML model achieves 27× lower MAE than this physics model on real data.

How to Increase PV Efficiency
1

Optimal Panel Tilt & Orientation

Face panels toward the equator (south in the northern hemisphere) and tilt them at an angle equal to your latitude. A correctly oriented panel captures up to 30% more energy than a flat panel. Solar trackers can add another 20–25% by following the sun throughout the day.

+25–30%
2

Keep Panels Clean & Dust-Free

Dust, bird droppings, and grime can reduce output by 5–25% in dusty climates. Regular cleaning (monthly in arid regions) restores full performance. Hydrophobic anti-soiling coatings can reduce cleaning frequency. This dataset is from India where soiling is a real factor.

+5–25%
3

Manage Panel Temperature

For every 1 °C above 25 °C, mono-Si panels lose ~0.45% efficiency. At 55 °C that's a 13.5% loss. Use panels with good rear ventilation, bifacial modules (which run cooler), or active cooling in extreme climates. Avoid mounting flush against hot roofs.

+5–15%
4

Reduce Shading & Mismatch Losses

Even partial shading of one panel in a string can reduce the entire string's output. Use micro-inverters or DC optimisers (e.g., Enphase, SolarEdge) to isolate underperforming panels. String-level MPPT typically loses 10–15% compared to panel-level MPPT.

+10–15%
5

Use High-Efficiency Monocrystalline Panels

Panel technology matters: polycrystalline ~17%, monocrystalline ~22%, TOPCon ~24%, and perovskite tandem cells (emerging) exceed 30% in lab conditions. Upgrading older poly-Si panels to modern mono-Si can yield 5–8 percentage points more efficiency per m².

+5–8 pts
6

Upgrade to a High-Efficiency Inverter

Inverter efficiency ranges from ~93% (older) to 99% (modern SiC-based units). A 5% inverter efficiency improvement on a 1 MW plant saves ~50 kW of continuous output. Modern string inverters with multiple MPPT inputs also minimise mismatch losses between strings.

+2–6%
7

Predictive Maintenance with ML

This project's model (R² = 0.9865) can detect anomalies: if actual output deviates significantly from the predicted value, it flags a fault (dirty panel, failed string, inverter clipping). Early detection of a single failed string can prevent weeks of lost output. Integrating real-time prediction into SCADA systems is now industry standard.

Proactive
8

Battery Storage & Load Shifting

Excess midday generation that cannot be exported is wasted. Adding battery storage (lithium-ion, LFP) allows energy to be used during evening peak demand, effectively increasing the useful energy yield by 15–30%. Pair with ML forecasting to optimise charge/discharge cycles.

+15–30%
